## Root-cause and assumption delta

- **Root cause:** the original plan and implementation treated "later worktree delta" as
  current dirty state only. That plan deficiency allowed a correct implementation of the
  stated live-state query to omit committed post-baseline paths. The fix changes the
  boundary's evidence source, not merely the report symptom.
- **A-04 (reversible, fail-closed):** Git raw diff status `A/M/D/T`, with rename detection
  disabled, is the committed-path authority. A rename is therefore visible as delete plus
  add; malformed or unsupported raw records abort with `BOUNDARY_HISTORY_PARSE_ERROR`.
- The reviewed revision must resolve to an exact commit and descend from the baseline
  commit. A missing, unrelated, or moving revision aborts rather than choosing another
  range.
- No assumption authorizes editing the v2 baseline/allowlist, hiding `01-REVIEW.md`,
  changing the accepted bundle, granting external publication, or incorporating the
  independently non-blocking review findings.
